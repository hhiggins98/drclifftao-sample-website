The Approach

Subspecialty expertise
your patients deserve.

Most chiropractors are trained to take imaging. Far fewer are trained to read it with the depth a clinical decision requires. Dr. Tao built his practice on a single principle: every diagnostic image contains information that affects patient care, and it deserves the attention of a specialist who has spent two decades doing nothing else. His reports are detailed, actionable, and written to help referring doctors make better decisions — not just confirm what they already suspected.

1

Submit your imaging study — plain film, MRI, CT, or CBCT — through the practice portal or by phone. Standard reports are returned within 24 hours.

2

Receive a highly detailed interpretation written specifically for the referring clinician — findings, clinical correlations, and differential considerations included.

3

Stat reads available within 30 minutes for time-sensitive cases, with secondary opinions available for complex or disputed findings.

MRI Interpretation

MRI reports from general radiologists often lack the chiropractic and musculoskeletal clinical context your treatment decisions require. Dr. Tao reads spinal and MSK MRI with the specific lens of a DACBR — bridging the gap between the image and your plan of care.

Cross-Sectional
CT & CBCT Interpretation

Cone beam CT and conventional CT demand subspecialty-level reading to extract their full clinical value. Dr. Tao specialises in CBCT and CT interpretation for spine and musculoskeletal regions, with detailed reporting that supports documentation, billing, and referral decisions.
